# 2-minute demo script

Record from 1 December 2026 so the Live winter cards and wheat guesses are visible. Use a phone-sized
browser window. Site: <https://parimalgavali.github.io/crop-stress-ews/>.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Live map, NRW fields | "This warns when a winter-wheat field in North Rhine-Westphalia starts falling behind its neighbours. It runs on free satellite and weather data and updates every five days." |
| 0:15–0:40 | Tap a field the map rates as wheat; Live card | "It's December, so there's no spring risk yet. The system guesses from radar which fields are winter wheat and checks whether the crop came up and whether there's standing water." |
| 0:40–0:50 | Press read aloud, switch to German | "Everything is in plain words, in English and German, and can be read aloud. Colour is never the only signal." |
| 0:50–1:15 | Replay 2024, press play through April–June | "This is the held-out wet season 2024. Red alarms came a median of 15 days before the field fell behind. The AUROC was 0.74 to 0.80, a bit above a simple persistence baseline." |
| 1:15–1:35 | Check my field: tap 4 corners, Done | "Anyone can draw a field. The browser fetches the satellite images itself, with no server." |
| 1:35–1:50 | Phone home screen icon, airplane mode, reopen | "It installs like an app and shows the last data offline, out in the field." |
| 1:50–2:00 | README / forecast log | "From April, every forecast is saved before the outcome is known and scored afterwards. That's the real test. I also report the ideas that didn't work." |

**Don't claim:** yield forecasts, diagnoses, or that the GRU-D model is proven better.
