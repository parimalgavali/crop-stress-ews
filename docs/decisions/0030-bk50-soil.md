# ADR 0030: Soil from the NRW soil map (BK50) on Live cards, tested as a model input

Status: Accepted (US-33, Issue #73)

## Context
Waterlogging and dry-spell stress depend a lot on soil. The Geological Survey NRW's BK50
(dl-de/by-2-0) has, for every map unit, soil type, texture, available water capacity in the
root zone (nFK, mm), groundwater level (GW_STUFE) and waterlogging level (SW_STUFE). The full
GeoPackage is 640 MB. The WMS `https://www.wms.nrw.de/gd/bk050` answers GetFeatureInfo with all
attributes as GeoJSON in about 0.8 s.

## Decision
- `cropews.data.soil.field_soils` asks the WMS once per field, at its representative point,
  with 8 threads and 3 tries. There is no bulk download.
- Live: `_soils` keeps results in `data/live_cache/soil.parquet`. Soil doesn't change, so only
  fields new to the sample are asked. `latest.json.soil[field_id]` holds the result. The card
  line, for example "🟫 Soil (NRW soil map): Pseudogley, sandiger Lehm · holds a medium amount
  of water · tends to stay wet after rain", is EN/DE and read aloud.
  - Water held: nFK < 90 mm is "little", < 200 mm "medium", otherwise "lots".
  - Groundwater close to the surface: GW_STUFE 1–3 (the sample point had GW3 with groundwater at
    10.5 dm).
  - Tends to stay wet: SW_STUFE ≥ 3.
  - The class cut-offs are our plain-words reading, not GD NRW classes.
- Model input experiment (`scripts/soil_eval.py`, US-08 features + `nfk_mm`, `gw_level`,
  `sw_level`). All 1,321 training fields had soil data. AUROC without → with soil:

| Horizon | Model | 2022 validation | 2024 test | 2025 check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 d | logistic | 0.858 → 0.862 | 0.742 → 0.752 | 0.868 → 0.891 |
| 14 d | logistic | 0.784 → 0.784 | 0.795 → 0.796 | 0.879 → 0.909 |
| 21 d | logistic | 0.758 → 0.756 | 0.768 → 0.774 | 0.868 → 0.898 |
| 7 d | LightGBM | 0.878 → 0.876 | 0.761 → 0.762 | 0.846 → 0.858 |
| 14 d | LightGBM | 0.833 → 0.844 | 0.792 → 0.756 | 0.860 → 0.875 |
| 21 d | LightGBM | 0.823 → 0.823 | 0.737 → 0.714 | 0.823 → 0.843 |

- **Not adopted.** On 2022, the season that decides, the served logistic model doesn't change
  (±0.004). The 2025 gains (+0.02–0.03) are inside the bootstrap intervals (for example 14 d
  0.879 [0.824, 0.920] → 0.909 [0.870, 0.938]), and LightGBM drops on 2024.
- Soil is a candidate for the yearly champion/challenger check (ADR 0026), so it can come back
  if it wins there.

## Consequences
- With 1 request per new field, a full new sample of 360 fields takes about 1 minute. Later
  runs make almost no requests.
- If the WMS is down, the fields that failed get no soil line, and they are asked again on the
  next run.
